Soru 23–30: Başlangıç değer problemlerini çözünüz. ($x>0$ varsayınız.)

23.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}-2xy^{\prime}-10y=0,\ \ y(1)=5,\ y^{\prime}(1)=4$

24.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}-4xy^{\prime}+6y=0,\ \ y(2)=0,\ y^{\prime}(2)=4$

25.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}+5xy^{\prime}+3y=0,\ \ y(1)=1,\ y^{\prime}(1)=-5$

26.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}-2y=4x-8,\ \ y(1)=4,\ y^{\prime}(1)=-1$

27.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}-4xy^{\prime}+4y=4x^{2}-6x^{3},\ \ y(2)=4,\ y^{\prime}(2)=-1$

28.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}+2xy^{\prime}-6y=10x^{2},\ \ y(1)=1,\ y^{\prime}(1)=-6$

29.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}-5xy^{\prime}+8y=2x^{3},\ \ y(2)=0,\ y^{\prime}(2)=-8$

30.   $x^{2}y^{\prime\prime}-6y=\ln x,\ \ y(1)=\tfrac{1}{6},\ y^{\prime}(1)=-\tfrac{1}{6}$
