Sorular Alıştırmalar 4.3

Yöntem. Önce $y_{c}$ (tamamlayıcı fonksiyon) bulunur. Sağ taraftaki her terim için deneme fonksiyonu yazılır; deneme terimi $y_{c}$'de zaten varsa $x$'in uygun kuvvetiyle çarpılır.

Kısayol. Yardımcı polinom $p(m)$ olmak üzere, $e^{rx}$ tipi zorlamalarda

$$L\!\left[u(x)e^{rx}\right]=e^{rx}\left[p(r)u+p'(r)u'+\tfrac{p''(r)}{2!}u''+\cdots\right]$$

Bu formül $r$ kök olduğunda ($p(r)=0$) katsayı hesabını çok kısaltır.

Soru 1–34: Genel çözümü bulunuz.

1.   $y''-3y'+2y=4x^{2}$

$m^{2}-3m+2=(m-1)(m-2)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{2x}$

Deneme: $y_{p}=Ax^{2}+Bx+C$

$2A-3(2Ax+B)+2\left(Ax^{2}+Bx+C\right)$

$x^{2}$: $2A=4 \implies A=2$

$x$: $-6A+2B=0 \implies B=6$

sabit: $2A-3B+2C=0 \implies C=7$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{2x}+2x^{2}+6x+7$

2.   $y''-2y'-8y=4e^{2x}-21e^{-3x}$

$m^{2}-2m-8=(m-4)(m+2)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{4x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}$

$p(2)=4-4-8=-8$ → $-8A=4 \implies A=-\dfrac{1}{2}$

$p(-3)=9+6-8=7$ → $7B=-21 \implies B=-3$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{4x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}-\dfrac{1}{2}e^{2x}-3e^{-3x}$

3.   $y''+2y'+5y=6\sin 2x+7\cos 2x$

$m=-1\pm 2i$ → $y_{c}=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x\right)$

$e^{-x}$ çarpanı olduğundan $\cos 2x,\sin 2x$ tekrar değil.

$y_{p}=A\cos 2x+B\sin 2x$ → toplam $(A+4B)\cos 2x+(B-4A)\sin 2x$

$A+4B=7$,   $-4A+B=6$ → $B=2$,   $A=-1$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x\right)-\cos 2x+2\sin 2x$

4.   $y''+2y'+2y=10\sin 4x$

$m=-1\pm i$ → $y_{c}=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos x+c_{2}\sin x\right)$

$y_{p}=A\cos 4x+B\sin 4x$:

$\cos$: $-16A+8B+2A=-14A+8B=0$

$\sin$: $-16B-8A+2B=-8A-14B=10$

$B=\dfrac{7A}{4}$ → $-8A-\dfrac{49A}{2}=10 \implies A=-\dfrac{4}{13}$,   $B=-\dfrac{7}{13}$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos x+c_{2}\sin x\right)-\dfrac{4\cos 4x+7\sin 4x}{13}$

5.   $y''+2y'+4y=13\cos 4x$

$m=-1\pm i\sqrt{3}$ → $y_{c}=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos\sqrt{3}x+c_{2}\sin\sqrt{3}x\right)$

$y_{p}=A\cos 4x+B\sin 4x$:

$\cos$: $-12A+8B=13$    $\sin$: $-8A-12B=0 \implies A=-\dfrac{3B}{2}$

$18B+8B=26B=13 \implies B=\dfrac{1}{2}$,   $A=-\dfrac{3}{4}$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos\sqrt{3}x+c_{2}\sin\sqrt{3}x\right)-\dfrac{3}{4}\cos 4x+\dfrac{1}{2}\sin 4x$

6.   $y''-3y'-4y=16x-12e^{2x}$

$(m-4)(m+1)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{4x}+c_{2}e^{-x}$

$16x$: $y_{p}=Ax+B$ → $-4Ax+(-3A-4B)=16x$

$A=-4$,   $B=3$

$-12e^{2x}$: $p(2)=4-6-4=-6$ → $-6C=-12 \implies C=2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{4x}+c_{2}e^{-x}-4x+3+2e^{2x}$

7.   $y''+6y'+5y=2e^{x}+10e^{5x}$

$(m+1)(m+5)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{-x}+c_{2}e^{-5x}$

$p(1)=1+6+5=12$ → $12A=2 \implies A=\dfrac{1}{6}$

$p(5)=25+30+5=60$ → $60B=10 \implies B=\dfrac{1}{6}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{-x}+c_{2}e^{-5x}+\dfrac{1}{6}e^{x}+\dfrac{1}{6}e^{5x}$

8.   $y''+2y'+10y=5xe^{-2x}$

$m=-1\pm 3i$ → $y_{c}=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos 3x+c_{2}\sin 3x\right)$

$p(-2)=4-4+10=10$,   $p'(m)=2m+2 \implies p'(-2)=-2$

$y_{p}=(Ax+B)e^{-2x}$ → $\left[10(Ax+B)-2A\right]e^{-2x}=5xe^{-2x}$

$10A=5 \implies A=\dfrac{1}{2}$;   $10B-1=0 \implies B=\dfrac{1}{10}$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos 3x+c_{2}\sin 3x\right)+\dfrac{(5x+1)e^{-2x}}{10}$

9.   $2y''+3y'-2y=6x^{2}e^{x}-4x^{2}+12$

$2m^{2}+3m-2=(2m-1)(m+2)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x/2}+c_{2}e^{-2x}$

$6x^{2}e^{x}$: $y=ue^{x}$ konursa operatör $3u+7u'+2u''$ olur.

$u=Ax^{2}+Bx+C$:   $3Ax^{2}+(3B+14A)x+(3C+7B+4A)=6x^{2}$

$A=2$,   $B=-\dfrac{28}{3}$,   $C=\dfrac{172}{9}$

$-4x^{2}+12$: $y_{p}=Dx^{2}+Ex+F$

$-2Dx^{2}+(6D-2E)x+(4D+3E-2F)$ → $D=2$, $E=6$, $F=7$

Çözüm:

$$y=c_{1}e^{x/2}+c_{2}e^{-2x}+\left(2x^{2}-\tfrac{28}{3}x+\tfrac{172}{9}\right)e^{x}+2x^{2}+6x+7$$

10.   $y''+6y'+8y=6xe^{2x}+8x^{2}$

$(m+2)(m+4)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{-2x}+c_{2}e^{-4x}$

$6xe^{2x}$: $p(2)=24$,   $p'(2)=10$

$24A=6 \implies A=\dfrac{1}{4}$;   $24B+10\cdot\tfrac{1}{4}=0 \implies B=-\dfrac{5}{48}$

$8x^{2}$: $y_{p}=Cx^{2}+Dx+E$ → $8C=8$, $12C+8D=0$, $2C+6D+8E=0$

$C=1$,   $D=-\dfrac{3}{2}$,   $E=\dfrac{7}{8}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{-2x}+c_{2}e^{-4x}+\dfrac{(12x-5)e^{2x}}{48}+x^{2}-\dfrac{3x}{2}+\dfrac{7}{8}$

11.   $y''+4y=4\sin 2x+8\cos 2x$

$m=\pm 2i$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x$ — sağ taraf tekrar ediyor, $x$ ile çarpılır.

$y_{p}=x\left(A\cos 2x+B\sin 2x\right)$ için

$$y_{p}''+4y_{p}=-4A\sin 2x+4B\cos 2x$$

$-4A=4 \implies A=-1$;   $4B=8 \implies B=2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x+x\left(2\sin 2x-\cos 2x\right)$

12.   $y''-4y=16xe^{2x}$

$m=\pm 2$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}$;   $e^{2x}$ tekrar → $x$ ile çarp.

$y_{p}=\left(Ax^{2}+Bx\right)e^{2x}$;   $p(2)=0$, $p'(2)=4$, $\tfrac{p''}{2!}=1$

$4u'+u''=8Ax+4B+2A=16x$

$A=2$,   $B=-1$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}+\left(2x^{2}-x\right)e^{2x}$

13.   $4y''-4y'+y=e^{x/2}+e^{-x/2}$

$(2m-1)^{2}$ → $m=\dfrac{1}{2}$ çift kök;   $y_{c}=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x/2}$

$y=ue^{x/2}$ konursa operatör sadece $4u''e^{x/2}$ olur.

$e^{x/2}$: çift tekrar → $u=Ax^{2}$;   $4(2A)=1 \implies A=\dfrac{1}{8}$

$e^{-x/2}$: $p\!\left(-\tfrac{1}{2}\right)=1+2+1=4$ → $4B=1 \implies B=\dfrac{1}{4}$

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x/2}+\dfrac{x^{2}e^{x/2}}{8}+\dfrac{e^{-x/2}}{4}$

14.   $y''-6y'+9y=6e^{3x}+5xe^{4x}$

$(m-3)^{2}$ → $y_{c}=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{3x}$

$6e^{3x}$: çift tekrar. $y=ue^{3x}$ için operatör $u''e^{3x}$'tir.

$u=Ax^{2}$ → $2A=6 \implies A=3$

$5xe^{4x}$: $p(4)=16-24+9=1$,   $p'(4)=2$

$\left[1\cdot(Bx+C)+2B\right]=5x \implies B=5$,   $C=-10$

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{3x}+3x^{2}e^{3x}+(5x-10)e^{4x}$

15.   $y'''+4y''+y'-6y=-18x^{2}+1$

$m=1$ kök: $1+4+1-6=0$ → $(m-1)(m+2)(m+3)$

$y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}+c_{3}e^{-3x}$

$y_{p}=Ax^{2}+Bx+C$:

$8A+(2Ax+B)-6\left(Ax^{2}+Bx+C\right)$

$x^{2}$: $-6A=-18 \implies A=3$

$x$: $2A-6B=0 \implies B=1$

sabit: $8A+B-6C=1 \implies C=4$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}+c_{3}e^{-3x}+3x^{2}+x+4$

16.   $y'''+2y''-3y'-10y=8xe^{-2x}$

$m=2$ kök: $8+8-6-10=0$ → $(m-2)\left(m^{2}+4m+5\right)$

$m=-2\pm i$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{2x}+e^{-2x}\left(c_{2}\cos x+c_{3}\sin x\right)$

Dikkat: $-2$ reel kök değil (kökler $-2\pm i$), dolayısıyla tekrar yok.

$p(-2)=-8+8+6-10=-4$,   $p'(m)=3m^{2}+4m-3 \implies p'(-2)=1$

$\left[-4(Ax+B)+A\right]=8x \implies A=-2$,   $B=-\dfrac{1}{2}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{2x}+e^{-2x}\left(c_{2}\cos x+c_{3}\sin x\right)-\left(2x+\tfrac{1}{2}\right)e^{-2x}$

17.   $y'''+y''+3y'-5y=5\sin 2x+10x^{2}+3x+7$

$m=1$ kök → $(m-1)\left(m^{2}+2m+5\right)$,   $m=-1\pm 2i$

$y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x}+e^{-x}\left(c_{2}\cos 2x+c_{3}\sin 2x\right)$

$5\sin 2x$: $y_{p}=A\cos 2x+B\sin 2x$

$\cos$: $-9A-2B=0$    $\sin$: $2A-9B=5$

$B=-\dfrac{9A}{2}$ → $2A+\dfrac{81A}{2}=5 \implies A=\dfrac{2}{17}$,   $B=-\dfrac{9}{17}$

$10x^{2}+3x+7$: $y_{p}=Cx^{2}+Dx+E$

$-5C=10 \implies C=-2$;   $6C-5D=3 \implies D=-3$;   $2C+3D-5E=7 \implies E=-4$

Çözüm:

$$y=c_{1}e^{x}+e^{-x}\left(c_{2}\cos 2x+c_{3}\sin 2x\right)+\frac{2\cos 2x-9\sin 2x}{17}-2x^{2}-3x-4$$

18.   $4y'''-4y''-5y'+3y=3x^{3}-8x$

$m=\dfrac{1}{2}$ kök → $(2m-1)\left(2m^{2}-m-3\right)=(2m-1)(2m-3)(m+1)$

$y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x/2}+c_{2}e^{3x/2}+c_{3}e^{-x}$

$y_{p}=Ax^{3}+Bx^{2}+Cx+D$:

$x^{3}$: $3A=3 \implies A=1$

$x^{2}$: $-15A+3B=0 \implies B=5$

$x$: $-24A-10B+3C=-8 \implies C=22$

sabit: $24A-8B-5C+3D=0 \implies D=42$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x/2}+c_{2}e^{3x/2}+c_{3}e^{-x}+x^{3}+5x^{2}+22x+42$

19.   $y''+y'-6y=10e^{2x}-18e^{3x}-6x-11$

$(m-2)(m+3)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{-3x}$

$10e^{2x}$: $2$ kök! → $y_{p}=Axe^{2x}$;   $p'(2)=5$

$5A=10 \implies A=2$

$-18e^{3x}$: $p(3)=9+3-6=6$ → $6B=-18 \implies B=-3$

$-6x-11$: $-6Cx+(C-6D)$ → $C=1$,   $D=2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{-3x}+2xe^{2x}-3e^{3x}+x+2$

20.   $y''+y'-2y=6e^{-2x}+3e^{x}-4x^{2}$

$(m-1)(m+2)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}$;   $p'(m)=2m+1$

$6e^{-2x}$: $-2$ kök; $p'(-2)=-3$ → $-3A=6 \implies A=-2$

$3e^{x}$: $1$ kök; $p'(1)=3$ → $3B=3 \implies B=1$

$-4x^{2}$: $-2Cx^{2}+(2C-2D)x+(2C+D-2E)$

$C=2$,   $D=2$,   $E=3$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{-2x}-2xe^{-2x}+xe^{x}+2x^{2}+2x+3$

21.   $y''-6y'+5y=24x^{2}e^{x}+8e^{5x}$

$(m-1)(m-5)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{5x}$

$24x^{2}e^{x}$: $e^{x}$ tekrar → $u=Ax^{3}+Bx^{2}+Cx$

$y=ue^{x}$ için operatör $-4u'+u''$:

$-12Ax^{2}+(6A-8B)x+(2B-4C)=24x^{2}$

$A=-2$,   $B=-\dfrac{3}{2}$,   $C=-\dfrac{3}{4}$

$8e^{5x}$: $5$ kök; $p'(5)=4$ → $4D=8 \implies D=2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{5x}-\dfrac{\left(8x^{3}+6x^{2}+3x\right)e^{x}}{4}+2xe^{5x}$

22.   $y''-4y'+5y=6e^{2x}\cos x$

$m=2\pm i$ → $y_{c}=e^{2x}\left(c_{1}\cos x+c_{2}\sin x\right)$ — sağ taraf tam tekrar.

$y=e^{2x}w$ konursa denklem sadeleşir:

$$y''-4y'+5y=e^{2x}\left(w''+w\right)$$

Yani $w''+w=6\cos x$ çözülecek; $w=x(A\cos x+B\sin x)$ için $w''+w=-2A\sin x+2B\cos x$.

$A=0$,   $2B=6 \implies B=3$

Çözüm: $y=e^{2x}\left(c_{1}\cos x+c_{2}\sin x\right)+3xe^{2x}\sin x$

23.   $y'''-3y''+4y=4e^{x}-18e^{-x}$

$m^{3}-3m^{2}+4=(m+1)(m-2)^{2}$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{-x}+\left(c_{2}+c_{3}x\right)e^{2x}$

$4e^{x}$: $p(1)=1-3+4=2$ → $2A=4 \implies A=2$

$-18e^{-x}$: $-1$ basit kök; $p'(m)=3m^{2}-6m \implies p'(-1)=9$

$9B=-18 \implies B=-2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{-x}+\left(c_{2}+c_{3}x\right)e^{2x}+2e^{x}-2xe^{-x}$

24.   $y'''-2y''-y'+2y=9e^{2x}-8e^{3x}$

$m^{2}(m-2)-(m-2)=(m-2)(m-1)(m+1)$

$y_{c}=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{x}+c_{3}e^{-x}$

$9e^{2x}$: $2$ basit kök; $p'(m)=3m^{2}-4m-1 \implies p'(2)=3$

$3A=9 \implies A=3$

$-8e^{3x}$: $p(3)=27-18-3+2=8$ → $8B=-8 \implies B=-1$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{x}+c_{3}e^{-x}+3xe^{2x}-e^{3x}$

25.   $y'''+y'=2x^{2}+4\sin x$

$m^{3}+m=m\left(m^{2}+1\right)$ → kökler $0,\;\pm i$

$y_{c}=c_{1}+c_{2}\cos x+c_{3}\sin x$

$2x^{2}$: $m=0$ kök olduğundan polinom $x$ ile çarpılır: $y_{p}=Ax^{3}+Bx^{2}+Cx$

$y'''+y'=3Ax^{2}+2Bx+(6A+C)=2x^{2}$

$A=\dfrac{2}{3}$,   $B=0$,   $C=-4$

$4\sin x$: $\pm i$ kök → $y_{p}=x\left(D\cos x+E\sin x\right)$

Hesap sonucu: $y'''+y'=-2D\cos x-2E\sin x$

$D=0$,   $-2E=4 \implies E=-2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}+c_{2}\cos x+c_{3}\sin x+\dfrac{2x^{3}}{3}-4x-2x\sin x$

26.   $y^{(4)}-3y'''+2y''=3e^{-x}+6e^{2x}-6x$

$m^{2}(m-1)(m-2)$ → kökler $0$ (çift), $1$, $2$

$y_{c}=c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}e^{x}+c_{4}e^{2x}$

$3e^{-x}$: $p(-1)=1+3+2=6$ → $6A=3 \implies A=\dfrac{1}{2}$

$6e^{2x}$: $2$ basit kök; $p'(m)=4m^{3}-9m^{2}+4m \implies p'(2)=4$

$4B=6 \implies B=\dfrac{3}{2}$

$-6x$: $m=0$ çift kök → $x^{2}$ ile çarp: $y_{p}=Cx^{3}+Dx^{2}$

$12Cx+(4D-18C)=-6x$ → $C=-\dfrac{1}{2}$,   $D=-\dfrac{9}{4}$

Çözüm:

$$y=c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}e^{x}+c_{4}e^{2x}+\tfrac{1}{2}e^{-x}+\tfrac{3}{2}xe^{2x}-\tfrac{1}{2}x^{3}-\tfrac{9}{4}x^{2}$$

27.   $y'''-6y''+11y'-6y=xe^{x}-4e^{2x}+6e^{4x}$

$(m-1)(m-2)(m-3)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{2x}+c_{3}e^{3x}$

$p'(m)=3m^{2}-12m+11$,   $\dfrac{p''(m)}{2}=3m-6$

$xe^{x}$: $1$ basit kök; $p'(1)=2$, $\tfrac{p''(1)}{2}=-3$

$u=Ax^{2}+Bx$ ile $2u'-3u''=4Ax+2B-6A=x$

$A=\dfrac{1}{4}$,   $B=\dfrac{3}{4}$

$-4e^{2x}$: $p'(2)=-1$ → $-C=-4 \implies C=4$

$6e^{4x}$: $p(4)=64-96+44-6=6$ → $D=1$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{2x}+c_{3}e^{3x}+\dfrac{x(x+3)e^{x}}{4}+4xe^{2x}+e^{4x}$

28.   $y'''-4y''+5y'-2y=3x^{2}e^{x}-7e^{x}$

$m=1$ kök → $(m-1)^{2}(m-2)$;   $y_{c}=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x}+c_{3}e^{2x}$

Sağ taraf $\left(3x^{2}-7\right)e^{x}$;   $e^{x}$ çift tekrar → $x^{2}$ ile çarp.

$p(1)=p'(1)=0$,   $\dfrac{p''(1)}{2}=-1$,   $\dfrac{p'''}{6}=1$

$u=Ax^{4}+Bx^{3}+Cx^{2}$ ile operatör $-u''+u'''$:

$-12Ax^{2}+(24A-6B)x+(6B-2C)=3x^{2}-7$

$A=-\dfrac{1}{4}$,   $B=-1$,   $C=\dfrac{1}{2}$

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x}+c_{3}e^{2x}-\left(\tfrac{x^{4}}{4}+x^{3}-\tfrac{x^{2}}{2}\right)e^{x}$

29.   $y'''-4y''+4y'=24xe^{2x}+16+9e^{3x}$

$m(m-2)^{2}$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}+\left(c_{2}+c_{3}x\right)e^{2x}$

$24xe^{2x}$: $2$ çift kök → $x^{2}$ ile çarp; $u=Ax^{3}+Bx^{2}$

$p(2)=p'(2)=0$,   $\dfrac{p''(2)}{2}=2$ → operatör $2u''+u'''$

$12Ax+(4B+6A)=24x$ → $A=2$,   $B=-3$

$16$: $m=0$ basit kök → $y_{p}=Cx$;   $4C=16 \implies C=4$

$9e^{3x}$: $p(3)=27-36+12=3$ → $D=3$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}+\left(c_{2}+c_{3}x\right)e^{2x}+\left(2x^{3}-3x^{2}\right)e^{2x}+4x+3e^{3x}$

30.   $y'''-4y'=32xe^{2x}-24x^{2}$

$m(m-2)(m+2)$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}+c_{2}e^{2x}+c_{3}e^{-2x}$

$32xe^{2x}$: $2$ basit kök; $p'(m)=3m^{2}-4 \implies p'(2)=8$,   $\dfrac{p''(2)}{2}=6$

$u=Ax^{2}+Bx$ ile $8u'+6u''=16Ax+(8B+12A)=32x$

$A=2$,   $B=-3$

$-24x^{2}$: $m=0$ basit kök → $y_{p}=Cx^{3}+Dx^{2}+Ex$

$-12Cx^{2}-8Dx+(6C-4E)=-24x^{2}$ → $C=2$, $D=0$, $E=3$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}+c_{2}e^{2x}+c_{3}e^{-2x}+\left(2x^{2}-3x\right)e^{2x}+2x^{3}+3x$

31.   $y''+y=x\sin x$

$m=\pm i$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}\cos x+c_{2}\sin x$; sağ taraf tekrar → $x$ ile çarp.

$y_{p}=\left(Ax^{2}+Bx\right)\cos x+\left(Cx^{2}+Dx\right)\sin x$

$y=P\cos x+Q\sin x$ için genel formül:

$$y''+y=\left(P''+2Q'\right)\cos x+\left(Q''-2P'\right)\sin x$$

$\cos$: $2A+2(2Cx+D)=0$ → $C=0$,   $D=-A$

$\sin$: $2C-2(2Ax+B)=x$ → $A=-\dfrac{1}{4}$,   $B=C=0$,   $D=\dfrac{1}{4}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}\cos x+c_{2}\sin x+\dfrac{x\sin x-x^{2}\cos x}{4}$

32.   $y''+4y=12x^{2}-16x\cos 2x$

$m=\pm 2i$ → $y_{c}=c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x$

Soru 51–64: Yalnızca deneme fonksiyonunu (belirsiz katsayılı doğrusal kombinasyonu) kurunuz. Katsayıları hesaplamayınız.

Kural. Sağ taraftaki her terim için, o terimin türevlerinden doğan bağımsız fonksiyonlar kümesi yazılır. Bu kümenin herhangi bir üyesi $y_{c}$'de varsa, o kümenin tamamı $x^{k}$ ile çarpılır — burada $k$, ilgili kökün katlılığıdır.

Deneme: $y_{p}=x\left(A\cos x+B\sin x\right)+x\left(C\cos 3x+D\sin 3x\right)$

Not: Sadeleştirme yapılmasaydı $\sin x\sin 2x$ için doğru deneme kümesi görülemezdi — çarpım halindeki trigonometrik zorlamalarda ilk adım daima toplam-fark formülüdür.
