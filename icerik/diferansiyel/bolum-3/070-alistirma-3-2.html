Sorular Alıştırmalar 4.2

Yöntem. $y=e^{mx}$ konarak yardımcı (karakteristik) denklem elde edilir. Kök yapısına göre:

Farklı reel kök $m$ → $e^{mx}$

$k$ katlı reel kök $m$ → $e^{mx},\,xe^{mx},\dots,x^{k-1}e^{mx}$

Karmaşık kök $a\pm bi$ → $e^{ax}\cos bx,\;e^{ax}\sin bx$  ($k$ katlıysa her birine $x,\dots,x^{k-1}$ çarpanları eklenir)

Soru 1–36: Genel çözümleri bulunuz.

1.   $y''-5y'+6y=0$

$m^{2}-5m+6=(m-2)(m-3)=0 \implies m=2,\,3$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{3x}$

2.   $y''-2y'-3y=0$

$m^{2}-2m-3=(m-3)(m+1)=0 \implies m=3,\,-1$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{3x}+c_{2}e^{-x}$

3.   $4y''-12y'+5y=0$

$4m^{2}-12m+5=0 \implies m=\dfrac{12\pm\sqrt{144-80}}{8}=\dfrac{12\pm 8}{8}$

$m=\dfrac{5}{2},\;\dfrac{1}{2}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{5x/2}+c_{2}e^{x/2}$

4.   $3y''-14y'-5y=0$

$3m^{2}-14m-5=(3m+1)(m-5)=0 \implies m=5,\;-\dfrac{1}{3}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{5x}+c_{2}e^{-x/3}$

5.   $2y''+y'-6y=0$

$2m^{2}+m-6=(2m-3)(m+2)=0 \implies m=\dfrac{3}{2},\;-2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{3x/2}+c_{2}e^{-2x}$

6.   $2y''+3y'-2y=0$

$2m^{2}+3m-2=(2m-1)(m+2)=0 \implies m=\dfrac{1}{2},\;-2$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x/2}+c_{2}e^{-2x}$

7.   $4y''-4y'+y=0$

$4m^{2}-4m+1=(2m-1)^{2}=0 \implies m=\dfrac{1}{2}$  (çift kök)

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x/2}$

8.   $y''-4y'+4y=0$

$m^{2}-4m+4=(m-2)^{2}=0 \implies m=2$ (çift kök)

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{2x}$

9.   $y''+6y'+11y=0$

$m^{2}+6m+11=0 \implies m=\dfrac{-6\pm\sqrt{36-44}}{2}=-3\pm i\sqrt{2}$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-3x}\left(c_{1}\cos\sqrt{2}\,x+c_{2}\sin\sqrt{2}\,x\right)$

10.   $16y''+32y'+25y=0$

$16m^{2}+32m+25=0 \implies m=\dfrac{-32\pm\sqrt{1024-1600}}{32}=\dfrac{-32\pm 24i}{32}$

$m=-1\pm\dfrac{3}{4}i$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos\dfrac{3x}{4}+c_{2}\sin\dfrac{3x}{4}\right)$

11.   $y'''-3y''-y'+3y=0$

Gruplayarak çarpanlara ayıralım:

$m^{3}-3m^{2}-m+3=m^{2}(m-3)-(m-3)=(m-3)\left(m^{2}-1\right)$

$=(m-3)(m-1)(m+1) \implies m=3,\,1,\,-1$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{3x}+c_{2}e^{x}+c_{3}e^{-x}$

12.   $y'''-6y''+5y'+12y=0$

$m=-1$ deneyelim: $-1-6-5+12=0$ ✓

Bölme: $m^{3}-6m^{2}+5m+12=(m+1)\left(m^{2}-7m+12\right)=(m+1)(m-3)(m-4)$

$m=-1,\,3,\,4$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{-x}+c_{2}e^{3x}+c_{3}e^{4x}$

13.   $y'''-5y''+7y'-3y=0$

$m=1$: $1-5+7-3=0$ ✓

$m^{3}-5m^{2}+7m-3=(m-1)\left(m^{2}-4m+3\right)=(m-1)^{2}(m-3)$

$m=1$ (çift),   $m=3$

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x}+c_{3}e^{3x}$

14.   $4y'''+4y''-7y'+2y=0$

$m=\dfrac{1}{2}$:   $\dfrac{1}{2}+1-\dfrac{7}{2}+2=0$ ✓

$4m^{3}+4m^{2}-7m+2=(2m-1)\left(2m^{2}+3m-2\right)=(2m-1)^{2}(m+2)$

$m=\dfrac{1}{2}$ (çift),   $m=-2$

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x/2}+c_{3}e^{-2x}$

15.   $y'''-y''+y'-y=0$

$m^{3}-m^{2}+m-1=m^{2}(m-1)+(m-1)=(m-1)\left(m^{2}+1\right)$

$m=1,\;\pm i$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}\cos x+c_{3}\sin x$

16.   $y'''+4y''+5y'+6y=0$

$m=-3$: $-27+36-15+6=0$ ✓

$m^{3}+4m^{2}+5m+6=(m+3)\left(m^{2}+m+2\right)$

$m^{2}+m+2=0 \implies m=\dfrac{-1\pm i\sqrt{7}}{2}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{-3x}+e^{-x/2}\left(c_{2}\cos\dfrac{\sqrt{7}\,x}{2}+c_{3}\sin\dfrac{\sqrt{7}\,x}{2}\right)$

17.   $y''-8y'+16y=0$

$m^{2}-8m+16=(m-4)^{2}=0 \implies m=4$ (çift)

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{4x}$

18.   $4y''+4y'+y=0$

$4m^{2}+4m+1=(2m+1)^{2}=0 \implies m=-\dfrac{1}{2}$ (çift)

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{-x/2}$

19.   $y''-4y'+13y=0$

$m=\dfrac{4\pm\sqrt{16-52}}{2}=2\pm 3i$

Çözüm: $y=e^{2x}\left(c_{1}\cos 3x+c_{2}\sin 3x\right)$

20.   $y''+6y'+25y=0$

$m=\dfrac{-6\pm\sqrt{36-100}}{2}=-3\pm 4i$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-3x}\left(c_{1}\cos 4x+c_{2}\sin 4x\right)$

21.   $y''+9y=0$

$m^{2}+9=0 \implies m=\pm 3i$  ($a=0$)

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}\cos 3x+c_{2}\sin 3x$

22.   $4y''+y=0$

$4m^{2}+1=0 \implies m=\pm\dfrac{i}{2}$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}\cos\dfrac{x}{2}+c_{2}\sin\dfrac{x}{2}$

23.   $y'''-6y''+12y'-8y=0$

Katsayılar $1,-6,12,-8$ → binom açılımı:

$m^{3}-6m^{2}+12m-8=(m-2)^{3}=0 \implies m=2$  (üç katlı)

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}x^{2}\right)e^{2x}$

24.   $8y'''+12y''+6y'+y=0$

$8m^{3}+12m^{2}+6m+1=(2m+1)^{3}=0 \implies m=-\dfrac{1}{2}$ (üç katlı)

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}x^{2}\right)e^{-x/2}$

25.   $y^{(4)}=0$

$m^{4}=0 \implies m=0$  (dört katlı)

$m=0$ kökü $e^{0x}=1$ verir; katlılık $x$ kuvvetlerini ekler:

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}x^{2}+c_{4}x^{3}$

Doğrudan da görülür: dördüncü türevi sıfır olan fonksiyonlar en fazla 3. dereceden polinomlardır.

26.   $y^{(4)}-y=0$

$m^{4}-1=\left(m^{2}-1\right)\left(m^{2}+1\right)=(m-1)(m+1)\left(m^{2}+1\right)$

$m=1,\,-1,\,\pm i$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{-x}+c_{3}\cos x+c_{4}\sin x$

27.   $y^{(4)}+8y''+16y=0$

$m^{4}+8m^{2}+16=\left(m^{2}+4\right)^{2}=0$

$m=\pm 2i$,   her biri çift katlı → $x$ çarpanlı terimler eklenir.

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)\cos 2x+\left(c_{3}+c_{4}x\right)\sin 2x$

28.   $y^{(4)}-y'''-3y''+y'+2y=0$

$m=1$: $1-1-3+1+2=0$ ✓ → bölünce $m^{3}-3m-2$

$m=-1$: $-1+3-2=0$ ✓ → bölünce $m^{2}-m-2=(m-2)(m+1)$

Toplam: $(m-1)(m+1)^{2}(m-2)$ → $m=1,\,2,\,-1$ (çift)

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{2x}+\left(c_{3}+c_{4}x\right)e^{-x}$

29.   $y^{(4)}-3y'''-2y''+2y'+12y=0$

$m=3$: $81-81-18+6+12=0$ ✓ → bölünce $m^{3}-2m-4$

$m=2$: $8-4-4=0$ ✓ → bölünce $m^{2}+2m+2$

$m^{2}+2m+2=0 \implies m=-1\pm i$

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}e^{3x}+c_{2}e^{2x}+e^{-x}\left(c_{3}\cos x+c_{4}\sin x\right)$

30.   $y^{(4)}+6y'''+15y''+20y'+12y=0$

$m=-2$: $16-48+60-40+12=0$ ✓ → bölünce $m^{3}+4m^{2}+7m+6$

$m=-2$ yine kök: $-8+16-14+6=0$ ✓ → bölünce $m^{2}+2m+3$

$m^{2}+2m+3=0 \implies m=-1\pm i\sqrt{2}$

$m=-2$ çift katlı.

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{-2x}+e^{-x}\left(c_{3}\cos\sqrt{2}\,x+c_{4}\sin\sqrt{2}\,x\right)$

31.   $y^{(5)}-2y^{(4)}+y'''=0$

$m^{5}-2m^{4}+m^{3}=m^{3}\left(m^{2}-2m+1\right)=m^{3}(m-1)^{2}$

$m=0$ (üç katlı),   $m=1$ (çift)

Çözüm: $y=c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}x^{2}+\left(c_{4}+c_{5}x\right)e^{x}$

32.   $y^{(5)}+5y^{(4)}+10y'''+10y''+5y'+y=0$

Katsayılar $1,5,10,10,5,1$ — Pascal üçgeninin 5. satırı:

$m^{5}+5m^{4}+10m^{3}+10m^{2}+5m+1=(m+1)^{5}=0$

$m=-1$,   beş katlı.

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}x^{2}+c_{4}x^{3}+c_{5}x^{4}\right)e^{-x}$

33.   $y^{(6)}+3y^{(4)}+3y''+y=0$

$m^{6}+3m^{4}+3m^{2}+1=\left(m^{2}+1\right)^{3}=0$

$m=\pm i$,   her biri üç katlı.

Çözüm: $y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x+c_{3}x^{2}\right)\cos x+\left(c_{4}+c_{5}x+c_{6}x^{2}\right)\sin x$

34.   $y^{(6)}-2y'''+y=0$

$m^{6}-2m^{3}+1=\left(m^{3}-1\right)^{2}=\left[(m-1)\left(m^{2}+m+1\right)\right]^{2}$

$=(m-1)^{2}\left(m^{2}+m+1\right)^{2}$

$m=1$ (çift);   $m^{2}+m+1=0 \implies m=-\dfrac{1}{2}\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}i$ (her biri çift)

Çözüm:

$$y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x}+e^{-x/2}\left[\left(c_{3}+c_{4}x\right)\cos\frac{\sqrt{3}\,x}{2}+\left(c_{5}+c_{6}x\right)\sin\frac{\sqrt{3}\,x}{2}\right]$$

35.   $y^{(4)}+y=0$

$m^{4}=-1$. Kutupsal biçimde $-1=e^{i(\pi+2k\pi)}$,   $|m|=1$:

$m=e^{i(\pi+2k\pi)/4}$,   $k=0,1,2,3$ → açılar $\dfrac{\pi}{4},\dfrac{3\pi}{4},\dfrac{5\pi}{4},\dfrac{7\pi}{4}$

$m=\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}i$  (dört işaret bileşimi)

Çözüm:

$$y=e^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x}\left(c_{1}\cos\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x+c_{2}\sin\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x\right)+e^{-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x}\left(c_{3}\cos\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x+c_{4}\sin\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x\right)$$

36.   $y^{(6)}+64y=0$

$m^{6}=-64$.   $|m|=64^{1/6}=2$, açılar $\dfrac{\pi+2k\pi}{6}$:

$\dfrac{\pi}{6},\;\dfrac{\pi}{2},\;\dfrac{5\pi}{6},\;\dfrac{7\pi}{6},\;\dfrac{3\pi}{2},\;\dfrac{11\pi}{6}$

Kökler:   $\pm 2i$,   $\sqrt{3}\pm i$,   $-\sqrt{3}\pm i$

Çözüm:

$$y=c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x+e^{\sqrt{3}x}\left(c_{3}\cos x+c_{4}\sin x\right)+e^{-\sqrt{3}x}\left(c_{5}\cos x+c_{6}\sin x\right)$$

Soru 37–56: Başlangıç değer problemlerini çözünüz.

37.   $y''-y'-12y=0$,   $y(0)=3$,   $y'(0)=5$

$m^{2}-m-12=(m-4)(m+3) \implies y=c_{1}e^{4x}+c_{2}e^{-3x}$

$c_{1}+c_{2}=3$,   $4c_{1}-3c_{2}=5$

$4c_{1}-3\left(3-c_{1}\right)=5 \implies 7c_{1}=14 \implies c_{1}=2$,   $c_{2}=1$

Çözüm: $y=2e^{4x}+e^{-3x}$

38.   $y''+7y'+10y=0$,   $y(0)=-4$,   $y'(0)=2$

$m^{2}+7m+10=(m+2)(m+5) \implies y=c_{1}e^{-2x}+c_{2}e^{-5x}$

$c_{1}+c_{2}=-4$,   $-2c_{1}-5c_{2}=2$

$-2\left(-4-c_{2}\right)-5c_{2}=8-3c_{2}=2 \implies c_{2}=2$,   $c_{1}=-6$

Çözüm: $y=-6e^{-2x}+2e^{-5x}$

39.   $y''-6y'+8y=0$,   $y(0)=1$,   $y'(0)=6$

$m^{2}-6m+8=(m-2)(m-4) \implies y=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}e^{4x}$

$c_{1}+c_{2}=1$,   $2c_{1}+4c_{2}=6 \implies c_{1}+2c_{2}=3$

Çıkarma: $c_{2}=2$,   $c_{1}=-1$

Çözüm: $y=2e^{4x}-e^{2x}$

40.   $3y''+4y'-4y=0$,   $y(0)=2$,   $y'(0)=-4$

$3m^{2}+4m-4=(3m-2)(m+2) \implies m=\dfrac{2}{3},\,-2$

$y=c_{1}e^{2x/3}+c_{2}e^{-2x}$

$c_{1}+c_{2}=2$,   $\dfrac{2}{3}c_{1}-2c_{2}=-4 \implies c_{1}-3c_{2}=-6$

Çıkarma: $4c_{2}=8 \implies c_{2}=2$,   $c_{1}=0$

Çözüm: $y=2e^{-2x}$

41.   $y''+6y'+9y=0$,   $y(0)=2$,   $y'(0)=-3$

$(m+3)^{2}=0 \implies y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{-3x}$

$y(0)=c_{1}=2$

$y'=\left[c_{2}-3\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)\right]e^{-3x}$ → $y'(0)=c_{2}-3c_{1}=-3 \implies c_{2}=3$

Çözüm: $y=(2+3x)e^{-3x}$

42.   $4y''-12y'+9y=0$,   $y(0)=4$,   $y'(0)=9$

$(2m-3)^{2}=0 \implies m=\dfrac{3}{2}$ (çift)

$y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{3x/2}$,   $c_{1}=4$

$y'(0)=c_{2}+\dfrac{3}{2}c_{1}=9 \implies c_{2}=9-6=3$

Çözüm: $y=(4+3x)e^{3x/2}$

43.   $y''+4y'+4y=0$,   $y(0)=3$,   $y'(0)=7$

$(m+2)^{2}=0 \implies y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{-2x}$

$c_{1}=3$;   $y'(0)=c_{2}-2c_{1}=7 \implies c_{2}=13$

Çözüm: $y=(3+13x)e^{-2x}$

44.   $9y''-6y'+y=0$,   $y(0)=3$,   $y'(0)=-1$

$(3m-1)^{2}=0 \implies m=\dfrac{1}{3}$ (çift)

$y=\left(c_{1}+c_{2}x\right)e^{x/3}$,   $c_{1}=3$

$y'(0)=c_{2}+\dfrac{c_{1}}{3}=-1 \implies c_{2}=-2$

Çözüm: $y=(3-2x)e^{x/3}$

45.   $y''-4y'+29y=0$,   $y(0)=0$,   $y'(0)=5$

$m=\dfrac{4\pm\sqrt{16-116}}{2}=2\pm 5i$

$y=e^{2x}\left(c_{1}\cos 5x+c_{2}\sin 5x\right)$;   $y(0)=c_{1}=0$

$y=c_{2}e^{2x}\sin 5x$ → $y'(0)=5c_{2}=5 \implies c_{2}=1$

Çözüm: $y=e^{2x}\sin 5x$

46.   $y''+6y'+58y=0$,   $y(0)=-1$,   $y'(0)=5$

$m=\dfrac{-6\pm\sqrt{36-232}}{2}=-3\pm 7i$

$y=e^{-3x}\left(c_{1}\cos 7x+c_{2}\sin 7x\right)$;   $c_{1}=-1$

Genel kural: $y'(0)=a\,c_{1}+b\,c_{2}$  (burada $a=-3$, $b=7$)

$3+7c_{2}=5 \implies c_{2}=\dfrac{2}{7}$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-3x}\left(-\cos 7x+\dfrac{2}{7}\sin 7x\right)$

47.   $y''+6y'+13y=0$,   $y(0)=3$,   $y'(0)=-1$

$m=-3\pm 2i$;   $y=e^{-3x}\left(c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x\right)$

$c_{1}=3$;   $-3c_{1}+2c_{2}=-1 \implies -9+2c_{2}=-1 \implies c_{2}=4$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-3x}\left(3\cos 2x+4\sin 2x\right)$

48.   $y''+2y'+5y=0$,   $y(0)=2$,   $y'(0)=6$

$m=-1\pm 2i$;   $y=e^{-x}\left(c_{1}\cos 2x+c_{2}\sin 2x\right)$

$c_{1}=2$;   $-c_{1}+2c_{2}=6 \implies c_{2}=4$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x}\left(2\cos 2x+4\sin 2x\right)$

49.   $9y''+6y'+5y=0$,   $y(0)=6$,   $y'(0)=0$

$m=\dfrac{-6\pm\sqrt{36-180}}{18}=\dfrac{-6\pm 12i}{18}=-\dfrac{1}{3}\pm\dfrac{2}{3}i$

$y=e^{-x/3}\left(c_{1}\cos\dfrac{2x}{3}+c_{2}\sin\dfrac{2x}{3}\right)$;   $c_{1}=6$

$-\dfrac{1}{3}(6)+\dfrac{2}{3}c_{2}=0 \implies c_{2}=3$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x/3}\left(6\cos\dfrac{2x}{3}+3\sin\dfrac{2x}{3}\right)$

50.   $4y''+4y'+37y=0$,   $y(0)=2$,   $y'(0)=-4$

$m=\dfrac{-4\pm\sqrt{16-592}}{8}=\dfrac{-4\pm 24i}{8}=-\dfrac{1}{2}\pm 3i$

$y=e^{-x/2}\left(c_{1}\cos 3x+c_{2}\sin 3x\right)$;   $c_{1}=2$

$-\dfrac{1}{2}(2)+3c_{2}=-4 \implies c_{2}=-1$

Çözüm: $y=e^{-x/2}\left(2\cos 3x-\sin 3x\right)$

51.   $y'''-6y''+11y'-6y=0$,   $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=0$, $y''(0)=2$

$m^{3}-6m^{2}+11m-6=(m-1)(m-2)(m-3)$

$y=c_{1}e^{x}+c_{2}e^{2x}+c_{3}e^{3x}$

$c_{1}+c_{2}+c_{3}=0$

$c_{1}+2c_{2}+3c_{3}=0$

$c_{1}+4c_{2}+9c_{3}=2$

Ardışık çıkarmalar: $c_{2}+2c_{3}=0$  ve  $2c_{2}+6c_{3}=2$

$c_{3}=1$,   $c_{2}=-2$,   $c_{1}=1$

Çözüm: $y=e^{x}-2e^{2x}+e^{3x}$

52.   $y'''-2y''+4y'-8y=0$,   $y(0)=2$, $y'(0)=0$, $y''(0)=0$

$m^{3}-2m^{2}+4m-8=m^{2}(m-2)+4(m-2)=(m-2)\left(m^{2}+4\right)$

$y=c_{1}e^{2x}+c_{2}\cos 2x+c_{3}\sin 2x$

$y(0)=c_{1}+c_{2}=2$

$y'(0)=2c_{1}+2c_{3}=0 \implies c_{3}=-c_{1}$

$y''(0)=4c_{1}-4c_{2}=0 \implies c_{2}=c_{1}$

Birinciden $2c_{1}=2 \implies c_{1}=c_{2}=1$,   $c_{3}=-1$

Çözüm: $y=e^{2x}+\cos 2x-\sin 2x$

53.   $y'''-3y''+4y=0$,   $y(0)=1$, $y'(0)=-8$, $y''(0)=-4$

$m=-1$: $-1-3+4=0$ ✓ → $m^{3}-3m^{2}+4=(m+1)\left(m^{2}-4m+4\right)=(m+1)(m-2)^{2}$

$y=c_{1}e^{-x}+\left(c_{2}+c_{3}x\right)e^{2x}$

$y(0)=c_{1}+c_{2}=1$

$y'(0)=-c_{1}+2c_{2}+c_{3}=-8$

$y''(0)=c_{1}+4c_{2}+4c_{3}=-4$

1.+2.:   $3c_{2}+c_{3}=-7$    3.−1.:   $3c_{2}+4c_{3}=-5$

Çıkarma: $3c_{3}=2 \implies c_{3}=\dfrac{2}{3}$,   $c_{2}=-\dfrac{23}{9}$,   $c_{1}=\dfrac{32}{9}$

Çözüm: $y=\dfrac{1}{9}\left[32e^{-x}+(6x-23)e^{2x}\right]$

54.   $y'''-5y''+9y'-5y=0$,   $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=1$, $y''(0)=6$

$m=1$: $1-5+9-5=0$ ✓ → $(m-1)\left(m^{2}-4m+5\right)$

$m^{2}-4m+5=0 \implies m=2\pm i$

$y=c_{1}e^{x}+e^{2x}\left(c_{2}\cos x+c_{3}\sin x\right)$

$y(0)=c_{1}+c_{2}=0$

$y'(0)=c_{1}+2c_{2}+c_{3}=1$

$y''(0)=c_{1}+3c_{2}+4c_{3}=6$

$c_{1}=-c_{2}$ konursa: $c_{2}+c_{3}=1$  ve  $2c_{2}+4c_{3}=6$
